You are now a critical Senior Product Designer reviewing your own implementation.

Do NOT assume the current design is good.

Critically inspect the entire website.

Ask:

1. Does this look like a real church website or a generic AI-generated template?
2. Is there a clear visual identity?
3. Is the typography intentional?
4. Is the color system coherent?
5. Is the spacing consistent?
6. Is the visual hierarchy clear?
7. Are there too many cards?
8. Are there unnecessary rounded containers?
9. Are there unnecessary borders?
10. Are there unnecessary shadows?
11. Is there too much whitespace?
12. Is there too little whitespace?
13. Are sections visually connected?
14. Are CTAs clear?
15. Does the navigation feel natural?
16. Does mobile feel intentionally designed?
17. Are components reused consistently?
18. Does every page feel like the same website?
19. Does the design communicate warmth and community?
20. Does the design feel timeless rather than trendy?

Find the 10 most important visual problems.

Then fix them.

Do not add new functionality.

Do not change business logic.

Do not introduce new visual patterns unless they belong to the established Design System.
